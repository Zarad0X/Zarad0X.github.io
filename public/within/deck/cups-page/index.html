Page of Cups

Within’s interpretation

A surprising reaction can be a useful beginning. The Page of Cups invites you to stay curious about a feeling, image, or idea that seems a little odd. Try writing it down, sketching it, or asking what made you notice it. Let the first experiment stay small and playful.

Which small surprise would be fun to follow for ten minutes?

Symbol notes

The fish
An unexpected detail worth being curious about.
The held cup
A feeling you can examine gently and at close range.
The shore
A place where an inner impression can meet an ordinary action.

Traditional meaning

In Waite’s description a fish appears from the cup. The court card is associated with a message, study, and reflection. The surprising image makes curiosity part of the encounter.
