Seven of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

When work grows slowly, pause where you can see it clearly. Look for a concrete sign of progress and one change you might make to how you tend it. Give yourself a date to check again.

What would count as progress at your next check-in?

Symbol notes

The growing plant
Some results develop on their own timetable.
The resting tool
Observation can be part of the work.
The seven coins
Look for what has accumulated so far.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a person leaning on a tool and looking at a plant bearing seven pentacles. He records divergent meanings, many concerned with practical affairs.
