Within’s interpretation

An interesting question can send your attention in every direction. Choose one observation to check and listen to the answer before preparing your reply. Curiosity works best when it has somewhere to land.

Symbol notes

The sword held high
Attention ready to engage.
The gusts
Signals that may change as you watch.
The uneven ground
Keep an eye on what is directly in front of you.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes an alert young figure moving through rough ground with a sword in both hands. He links the card with vigilance and examination.
