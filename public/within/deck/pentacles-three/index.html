Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Three of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

A skill becomes useful to a group when people can see how it fits. Show a small part of your work, ask how it meets the shared goal, and listen for what another person knows better.

What part of a shared project could you make easier to see?

Symbol notes

The arch
A whole structure that depends on its pieces.
The workbench
A visible place to demonstrate a skill.
The plans
A shared reference for making decisions together.

Traditional meaning

Waite shows a sculptor working in a monastery. He distinguishes the practiced craftsperson here from the learner of the Eight.
