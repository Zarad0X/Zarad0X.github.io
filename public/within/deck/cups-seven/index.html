Seven of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Several possibilities can feel equally convincing while they are still in your head. The Seven of Cups invites you to put one of them into words, then ask what you can actually observe or try. Imagination can widen the field. A small test can show which possibility is ready for your attention.

Which possibility could you test without committing to it yet?

Symbol notes

Seven vessels
Different possibilities can call for attention at once.
The floating shapes
An imagined choice still has details to discover.
The observer
Take enough distance to decide what to examine first.

Traditional meaning

Waite associates the cups in clouds with imagination and images of contemplation. Their contents have different attractions, and their place above the ground keeps the scene suspended between imagining and acting.
