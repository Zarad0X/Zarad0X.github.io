Seven of Swords

Within’s interpretation

An indirect plan may feel easier in the moment. Ask what it depends on and what would happen if other people learned how it works. Consider whether you can be clearer about the part that affects them.

What assumption is your plan relying on?

Symbol notes

The carried swords
What a strategy is trying to secure.
The two left behind
A plan has limits and leaves traces.
The distant camp
People affected by choices made out of sight.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a figure carrying five blades away from a camp, with two left behind. He records a mixture of intention, confidence and the possibility of a plan failing.
