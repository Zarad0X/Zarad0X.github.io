Three of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Some good things become more real when someone shares them with you. The Three of Cups invites you to notice a contribution worth thanking, a friend worth calling, or an ordinary milestone worth marking. Let the gathering fit the people in it. A short conversation can be enough.

Who helped make a recent good moment possible, and how could you thank them?

Symbol notes

Raised cups
Acknowledging a moment together gives it weight.
Three figures
Each person brings something different to the gathering.
Leaves below
Ordinary growth is worth noticing.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes cups raised together and associates the scene with a happy conclusion, comfort, and recovery. The people make the moment something they can share.
