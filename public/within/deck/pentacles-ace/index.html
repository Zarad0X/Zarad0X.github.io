Ace of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

A possibility gets easier to work with when you give it a place and a time. Take stock of what is already available, then choose one thing you could make, learn, or set up with it.

What do you already have that would let you begin?

Symbol notes

The offered coin
A resource you can put to use.
The garden gate
A concrete point of entry.
The path
Something grows once you start tending it.

Traditional meaning

Waite depicts a hand holding a pentacle out of a cloud. His account associates the card with contentment and favorable material conditions.
