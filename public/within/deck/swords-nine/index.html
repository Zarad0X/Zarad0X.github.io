Nine of Swords

Within’s interpretation

A worry can fill a whole room at night. Put its most specific sentence on paper and decide whether it needs comfort, a question answered, or attention tomorrow. You can handle those needs separately.

What would you want a trusted person to understand about this worry?

Symbol notes

The bed
A private place where concerns can become vivid.
The hands
A moment to acknowledge how much you are holding.
The parallel swords
Thoughts that return in a familiar pattern.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a figure sitting on a bed in sorrow beneath a row of swords. The scene centers on distress as it is experienced by the person in it.
