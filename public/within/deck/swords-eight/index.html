Eight of Swords

Within’s interpretation

When every option seems blocked, separate the barriers you can verify from the ones you are guessing about. Look for one small move you can make safely, or ask someone to help you see the space around you.

Which limitation can you describe in observable terms?

Symbol notes

The loose binding
One restriction may have room to change.
The standing swords
Some obstacles deserve careful attention.
The opening
Start with the room that is available.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a blindfolded, bound figure surrounded by swords. He characterizes the confinement as temporary.
