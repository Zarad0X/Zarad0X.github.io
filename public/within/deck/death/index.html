Death

Within’s interpretation

Something can have mattered and still be ready to end. Death invites you to notice a role, expectation, or arrangement that no longer fits. Letting go may include grief as well as relief; neither needs to be rushed. Consider what you want to honor, what you can release, and what support would make a transition more humane.

What could you let end while still honoring what it gave you?

Symbol notes

The rider
Change that moves beyond a familiar role or arrangement.
The white flower on the banner
The possibility of new life within a process of release.
The rising sun
Continuity beyond the edge of what is currently familiar.

Traditional meaning

The Death card is widely read as an image of endings and transformation. Its rider, banner, and distant rising sun place an ending within a larger cycle. In this app it is a symbolic prompt about change; it does not predict a death, illness, or other literal event.
