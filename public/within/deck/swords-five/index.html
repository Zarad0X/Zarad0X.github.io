Five of Swords

Within’s interpretation

After a disagreement, the last word may matter less than what happens next. Look at what each person walked away with. You can decide whether to repair a part of the exchange or end it with a clear boundary.

What did you hope to protect during the disagreement?

Symbol notes

The collected blades
What one person claims after the encounter.
The departing figures
Other people leave with their own account.
The fallen swords
The part of a dispute everyone can stop carrying.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes one figure collecting swords while two others leave the field. The scene brings the aftermath of conflict into view.
