Three of Swords

Within’s interpretation

A painful change can be difficult to describe while it is still happening. Give yourself time to name what you miss and what you need now. Even one honest sentence may be enough for today.

Which part of the loss is hardest to put into words?

Symbol notes

The heart
Something mattered enough to leave a mark.
The blades
Several parts of an experience may hurt at once.
The rain
Grief can take up space for a while.

Traditional meaning

Waite draws attention to the rain and the three blades piercing a heart. The meanings he records include separation and absence.
