Page of Wands

Within’s interpretation

You can learn something by giving a fresh interest a voice. The Page of Wands invites you to make a small attempt you can show or talk about. It can be unfinished. Notice what questions it brings back to you, and let one of them guide what you try next.

What small first attempt could you show to someone you trust?

Symbol notes

The upright wand
Bring a new interest into the open long enough to examine it.
The turned face
An attentive beginning can make room for a response.
The low dunes
A simple setting leaves room for something to be tried.

Traditional meaning

Waite links the Page with a messenger and describes a figure in the act of making a proclamation. The upright, leafy staff gives that announcement a visible point of attention.
