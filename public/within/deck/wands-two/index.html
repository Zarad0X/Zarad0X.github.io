Wands · Rider–Waite–Smith

Two of Wands

Within’s interpretation

Stand still long enough to notice what your current position makes possible. The Two of Wands invites you to compare the place you know with one direction you could explore. Give that direction a concrete detail, such as a conversation to have or a place to visit. You can gather information before committing.

What could you learn about a possible direction before deciding on it?

Symbol notes

The terrace
The position you already have can help you see farther.
The globe
An imagined direction can become specific enough to explore.
Two upright wands
A familiar foothold and a possible departure can be considered together.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a figure holding a globe on a high terrace and records sharply different readings of the card. The scene gives Within a way to ask how having a wide view affects what you want to do next.
