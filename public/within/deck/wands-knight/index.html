Knight of Wands

Within’s interpretation

Enthusiasm can take you a long way when it has somewhere to stop and check in. The Knight of Wands invites you to pick an action that matches your energy and your available time. Decide where you will pause to see what it changed before you ride on.

Where could you use your momentum today, and when will you pause to check it?

Symbol notes

The lifting horse
Energy is available to start moving.
The angled staff
Choose where to point that energy.
The open track
Pick a place to check in before the pace decides for you.

Traditional meaning

Waite draws attention to the horse’s movement and associates the rider with departure and change of place. Within keeps the forward motion and gives the rider a stretch of open ground.
