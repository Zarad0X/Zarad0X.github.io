Five of Wands

Within’s interpretation

Several people can care about the same thing and still pull it in different directions. The Five of Wands invites you to ask what everyone is trying to protect or prove. Try naming one rule that would make the disagreement more useful, or one part of it you can turn into a small test.

Symbol notes

Five crossed wands
Different aims can meet without immediately fitting together.
The circle below
Shared limits can turn friction into practice.
The gaps between strokes
Even in conflict there may be room to change your approach.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a mock fight and also connects the card with strenuous competition. The crossed wands leave space to ask whether the struggle is practice, friction, or both.
