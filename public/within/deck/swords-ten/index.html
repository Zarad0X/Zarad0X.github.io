Ten of Swords

Within’s interpretation

When an effort has reached its limit, take time to name what actually ended. Then look for the first ordinary thing that needs care. Restoring a routine can come before knowing what this ending means.

What has ended, and what still needs your attention?

Symbol notes

The still figure
An effort has come to a stop.
The ten blades
The accumulated weight deserves acknowledgment.
The lit horizon
The next day can begin with ordinary care.

Traditional meaning

Waite’s scene shows a fallen figure beneath ten swords. He associates the image with pain and desolation. Within gives the horizon space alongside that weight.
