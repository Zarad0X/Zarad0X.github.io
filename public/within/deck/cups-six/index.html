Six of Cups

Within’s interpretation

A memory can offer a clue about something you still need. The Six of Cups invites you to revisit a familiar place, a way of playing, or a small kindness that stayed with you. Choose one part worth bringing into the present. Let it take a shape that fits who you are now.

What did you enjoy when you were younger that could fit your life now?

Symbol notes

The courtyard
A place remembered through the feeling it held.
The flower in a cup
A small gesture can carry something from the past.
The cups in front
The past holds more than the memory that first comes to mind.

Traditional meaning

Waite links this card with childhood and pleasures recalled from the past, while also recording a reading about unfamiliar surroundings. Flowers offered between the figures connect memory with a gesture in the present.
