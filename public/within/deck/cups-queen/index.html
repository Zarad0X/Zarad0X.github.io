Queen of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Listening closely can be a form of care. The Queen of Cups invites you to notice what someone is feeling, including yourself, and decide what needs an answer now. Give the parts that are still tender some privacy. Your attention can be generous while your boundaries stay clear.

Which feeling needs a patient listener, and what boundary would make that listening possible?

Symbol notes

The lidded cup
A feeling can be held carefully before it is shared.
The arched seat
A boundary that gives quiet attention somewhere to rest.
Water at the feet
You can stay close to a feeling without being carried away by it.

Traditional meaning

Waite associates the Queen with perceptive care and a richly formed cup. The seated figure studies the vessel at the water’s edge. Within reads that attention as something available to anyone.
