Nine of Wands

Within’s interpretation

Experience may have taught you to stay alert. The Nine of Wands invites you to notice which precautions still help and where you can let the strain ease. Decide what support, rest, or clearer limit would make the next stretch sustainable.

What would let you stay prepared without staying tense all day?

Symbol notes

The held staff
Use the support you already know works.
The line behind
Past effort has built some protection around you.
The head wrap
What you have carried can affect what you need next.

Traditional meaning

Waite’s figure holds a staff before eight others and appears ready for opposition. His account also allows for delays and pauses. The scene holds both preparedness and the wear of staying ready.
