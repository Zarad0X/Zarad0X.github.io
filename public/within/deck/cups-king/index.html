King of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Steadiness can give everyone a little more room to think. The King of Cups asks you to name what you feel before deciding how to respond, especially when your choices affect other people. Try making one response clear and kind. You can return to the harder parts when you have more time.

What would a steady, kind response to this situation sound like?

Symbol notes

The raised seat
A steady vantage point from which to decide how to act.
The cup and scepter
Care and responsibility can guide the same response.
The small boat
Other lives keep moving around the choices you make.

Traditional meaning

Waite links this court card with responsibility, fair dealing, and creative intelligence. The seat above moving water gives a concrete image of staying present in changing conditions.
