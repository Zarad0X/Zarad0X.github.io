Queen of Wands

Within’s interpretation

You can make room for your own energy and for the people around you. The Queen of Wands invites you to notice where a more direct expression of interest would help. Say what excites you, invite someone in, or make one choice that lets your work be seen on your terms.

How could you let yourself be seen in a way that feels like you?

Symbol notes

The sunflower
Warmth you can show in a form that feels like yours.
The cat
A quiet presence that keeps its own pace.
The leafy staff
An interest that stays alive through attention.

Traditional meaning

Waite’s account emphasizes a magnetic presence and describes the suit as one of living, leafy wands. The sunflower and cat in the traditional scene give Within two different ways to picture warmth and independence.
