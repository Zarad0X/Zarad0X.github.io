Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Ten of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

Consider what you received from people who were here before you. A routine, a piece of knowledge, or a place of welcome can be worth passing on. Decide how to make one of those things easier for someone else to use.

What do you want to pass on in a form someone else can use?

Symbol notes

The archway
A place that can welcome people over time.
The elder and child
Knowledge can travel between generations.
The coins above the doorway
The traces of what has been built together.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes several generations and their dogs gathered beneath an arch. He connects the scene with household, family history and accumulated resources.
