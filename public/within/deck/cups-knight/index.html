Knight of Cups

Within’s interpretation

A sincere offer becomes clearer when it has a concrete form. The Knight of Cups invites you to move toward what matters with a pace the other person can respond to. Draft the invitation, make the gesture, or take a first step. Leave room for the answer to surprise you.

What invitation could you make clearly, with room for a real answer?

Symbol notes

The moving horse
A feeling starts to take shape through movement.
The open cup
A gesture someone else can freely respond to.
The water at the edge
The pace of the encounter matters as much as its intention.

Traditional meaning

Waite links the approaching rider to invitations, proposals, and messages. The cup is carried openly, making the offer visible before it arrives.
