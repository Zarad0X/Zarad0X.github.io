Four of Swords

Within’s interpretation

Try giving a problem a defined rest. You might close the tab, take a walk, or set a time to return tomorrow. A clear stopping point can make a pause easier to trust.

What could you set aside until a specific time?

Symbol notes

The resting figure
A deliberate break from effort.
The three mounted swords
Urgent thoughts can wait outside the pause.
The stone ledge
A stable place to put something down.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a knight’s resting effigy with hands in prayer. Retreat, solitude and repose are among the meanings he lists.
