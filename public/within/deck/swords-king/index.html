Within’s interpretation

A judgment becomes easier to trust when others can follow the reasoning. Name the evidence, the standard you are applying, and what might make you reconsider.

Symbol notes

Consistency across people and cases.
The steps
Decisions have effects beyond the seat of judgment.

Traditional meaning

Waite connects the seated sword bearer with judgment and the authority of an office. The scene gives that authority a visible weight.
