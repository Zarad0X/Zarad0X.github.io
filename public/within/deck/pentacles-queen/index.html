Queen of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

Look for one physical condition you could make kinder for yourself or someone else. A place to sit, food ready at the right time, or a little space to work can make care tangible.

What would make this space easier to live or work in?

Symbol notes

The coin in the lap
Care can attend to a concrete need.
The leafy canopy
A setting can offer shelter and ease.
The rabbit
Leave a little space for the lives around you.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a seated figure contemplating the pentacle. He records generosity, security and freedom among the card’s meanings.
