Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Two of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

When several demands change at once, look for a rhythm you can actually sustain. See which task can be made smaller, moved, or shared. Your schedule can move with you.

Which two things are you trying to keep in motion?

Symbol notes

The looping cord
Attention can move back and forth.
The two coins
Each demand has its own weight.
The changing waves
A schedule has to make room for changing conditions.

Traditional meaning

Waite depicts a dancer with two pentacles joined by a looping cord. He records both playfulness and the bustle that comes with obstacles.
