Ace of Wands

Within’s interpretation

There may be an idea you keep returning to even when nobody asks you to. The Ace of Wands invites you to give it a small beginning. Sketch a version, set out the tools, or spend ten minutes trying it. The first step can help you learn what kind of interest this is.

What could you begin in ten minutes with what you already have?

Symbol notes

The budding wand
An idea can be alive before it has a finished form.
The cloud
An impulse appearing while the larger picture is still unclear.
The distant ridge
A horizon you can approach one experiment at a time.

Traditional meaning

Waite places creation, enterprise, and beginnings around a wand offered from a cloud. In Within’s drawing, the leaves are the first signs that the idea has somewhere to grow.
