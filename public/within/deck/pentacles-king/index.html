King of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

When you look after shared resources, ask who can use them and how decisions get made. A simple routine or a clear record can make stewardship easier for the next person to carry.

What resource are you responsible for making easier to use?

Symbol notes

The bull heads
The patient strength behind a lasting structure.
The cultivated vines
Resources grow through continuing attention.
The coin at the center
Something held in trust for more than one use.

Traditional meaning

Waite notes the bull heads on the throne and the pentagram on the suit’s emblem. He connects the card with practical judgment and capability.
