Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Nine of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

Take some time to enjoy a resource or habit you helped build. Notice which conditions let you use it on your own terms and which ones you want to maintain.

What can you enjoy today because of work you did earlier?

Symbol notes

The vines
Comfort can take seasons of care to grow.
The perched bird
Trust and attention make freedom easier to hold.
The garden path
A place you can move through at your own pace.

Traditional meaning

Waite shows a figure with a bird on the wrist among abundant vines. His account includes discernment, accomplishment and security.
