Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Six of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

Before giving or accepting help, make room to talk about what would actually be useful. A clear offer with an easy way to decline can leave both people with more choice.

What kind of support would feel fair and useful here?

Symbol notes

The scales
Consider how the exchange feels from each side.
The offered coins
Help takes a form someone can actually use.
The open hands
Receiving is an active part of the exchange.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a merchant weighing money and distributing it to others. His account draws attention to gifts and the act of giving.
