Pentacles · Rider–Waite–Smith

Eight of Pentacles

Within’s interpretation

Pick one small part of a craft and practice it with attention. Compare a later attempt with an earlier one and name the difference you can actually see.

Which part of the work would repay focused repetition?

Symbol notes

The chisel
A specific tool for a specific improvement.
The unfinished piece
The attempt that has your attention now.
The displayed coins
A record of what repeated work has made possible.

Traditional meaning

Waite shows a stoneworker at work with completed pieces on display. He connects the scene with skill, employment and the early stages of craftsmanship.
