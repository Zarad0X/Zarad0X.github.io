Five of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Disappointment deserves some time before you try to make a lesson out of it. The Five of Cups lets you name what was lost and, when you are ready, notice what can still support you. Give both realities their own place. A small act of care can help you take the next step at your own pace.

What do you need to acknowledge about this loss, and what is still available to you?

Symbol notes

Three fallen cups
A loss can be named in its own terms.
Two standing cups
Some sources of support may still be available.
The bridge
A way forward can exist before you feel ready to take it.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes loss alongside what remains. Three cups have fallen and two are still standing. The bridge in the scene gives that contrast a direction to look toward.
