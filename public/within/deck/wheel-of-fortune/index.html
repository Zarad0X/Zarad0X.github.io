Wheel of Fortune

Within’s interpretation

Not every change is a verdict on your effort or worth. The Wheel invites you to distinguish what you can influence from what is moving around you. Look for a pattern without assuming it must repeat forever. A useful response may be a small adjustment, a backup plan, or the willingness to notice an opening you did not arrange.

What is changing that you could respond to without taking it as a judgment of yourself?

Symbol notes

The wheel
Conditions that change even when we wish they would stay still.
The rising and falling figures
A position can be temporary rather than a permanent identity.
The surrounding witnesses
The possibility of observing a pattern before responding.

Traditional meaning

The turning wheel is traditionally associated with changing circumstances and the rise and fall of fortune. Figures around it suggest different positions within the same movement. For this experience, the image offers a way to think about uncertainty and adaptation, rather than a claim that a fixed fate is directing events.
