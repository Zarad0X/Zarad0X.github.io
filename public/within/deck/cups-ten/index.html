Ten of Cups

Within’s interpretation

Belonging is often made out of repeated, ordinary acts. The Ten of Cups asks you to notice the people and places around whom you can breathe more easily. You might take care of one small ritual, offer a welcome, or make a little time for someone who makes life feel more inhabitable.

What small ritual helps a place or relationship feel like somewhere you belong?

Symbol notes

The arc of cups
Many small moments can form a shared sense of safety.
The open doorway
Belonging can begin with a gesture of welcome.
The two figures
A shared place can hold more than one way of being there.

Traditional meaning

Waite associates the arch of cups with contentment, love, and friendship, and allows for a reading connected with one’s home or community. Within places an open doorway under the arch.
