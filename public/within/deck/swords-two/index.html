Two of Swords

Within’s interpretation

Two priorities can both deserve your attention. Notice how much effort it takes to keep them balanced. There may be one piece of information you can seek before either has to give way.

Which uncertainty could you reduce this week?

Symbol notes

The blindfold
Some information is still out of view.
The balanced swords
Both sides require effort to hold.
Water behind the seat
Feelings can be present even when the surface looks still.

Traditional meaning

Waite describes a blindfolded figure balancing two swords and reads the image as a qualified kind of equilibrium.
